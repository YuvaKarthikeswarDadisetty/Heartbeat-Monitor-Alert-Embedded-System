|---|---|---|
| Arduino UNO firmware | PASS | Future |

| Clinical accuracy | Not applicable | Not applicable |

## Meaning

**PASS** means the behavior was observed in Wokwi.

**Future** means the behavior is designed but has not been physically validated.

This distinction is intentional and should be preserved in the project documentation.
